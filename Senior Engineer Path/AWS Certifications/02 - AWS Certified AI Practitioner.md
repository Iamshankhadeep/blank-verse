# AWS Certified AI Practitioner

> [!info] Level
> Foundational

> [!summary] Exam shape
> 65 questions, 90 minutes, passing score 700

## Who this is for
People entering AI on AWS who want a solid foundation before ML or GenAI specializations.

## Core focus areas
- AI, ML, and GenAI basics
- Bedrock, Amazon Q, SageMaker fundamentals
- prompt engineering basics
- responsible AI and governance
- core ML terminology

## Prep advice
- Do not treat this like an easy theory exam.
- Learn concepts, not just AWS product names.
- Be comfortable with common AI and GenAI terms.
