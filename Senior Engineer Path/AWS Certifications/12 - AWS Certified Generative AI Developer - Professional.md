# AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional

> [!info] Level
> Professional

> [!summary] Exam shape
> 75 questions, 130 minutes, passing score 750

## Who this is for
Engineers building production-grade GenAI applications on AWS.

## Core focus areas
- Bedrock and SageMaker
- prompt engineering
- vector embeddings
- RAG
- model evaluation
- governance, privacy, and responsible AI

## Prep advice
- Build at least one real GenAI app on AWS.
- Study end-to-end solution design, not isolated services.
- Understand safety, hallucination mitigation, privacy, and moderation trade-offs.

## Good next step
Pair with [[03 - AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate]] for stronger platform depth
