# AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate

> [!info] Level
> Associate

> [!summary] Exam shape
> 65 questions, 130 minutes, passing score 720

## Who this is for
Engineers who design, review, or reason about AWS architectures.

## Core focus areas
- architecture best practices
- secure and reliable systems
- compute, storage, and networking trade-offs
- cost-effective service selection
- scalability and resilience

## Prep advice
- Go deeper than Cloud Practitioner.
- Do hands-on labs with core services.
- Practice choosing the best option, not just a valid option.
